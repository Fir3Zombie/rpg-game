Zelda type rpg game. 
Work in progress.

W = move up
S = move down
A = move left
D = move right
space bar = attack
E = interact

You can interact with things like signs and some houses so that you can enter them.
If you can interact with something text will appear prompting you to "PRESS E TO READ/ENTER HOUSE"

There are two main enemys consisting of:

* slime 1 damage 100 Hp player deals 20 damage dies in 5 hits. This is a basic enemy and should be the first enemy the player fights in the story, they can be seen anywhere in the map and have so specific criteria for where they should spawn.

* Ghost enemy 2 damage to player 100 Hp player deals 15 damage takes 6 hits to kill, a more advanced enemy witch can cause lethal damage if the player isn't careful. But the fun part about this enemy is that the player can dodge its attacks by moving back before the attack lands.

player health consists of 1 big bar sliced into 6 pieces. Player has 12 Hp overall and 1 little bar counts towards 2 Hp 

Scenes:

Begging/village - This location holds 2 houses and 1 sign in it that the player can interact with. The scenes that this place consists of are the nurses house, abandoned house, and a sign outside the nurses house.

nurse house - This is where the nurse stays. By walking up to her you can interact with her and she will say hello to you. later on she will be able to heal you for a price.

abandoned house - this is where the ghost stays and it also makes your player tiny when you enter it. This is from the folklore in the story where the village people always talk about how the abandoned house is cursed. This location will take a big part in the story

World - this is the first scene made it has 2 slimes 2 signs that tell you the 2 directions/ other scenes you can go too. One says cliffside, and the other says cliff_path

cliffside - this location consists of not much, It mainly stands as a way for me to test new enemy's, this location was made for me to test how it would work for me to add more locations to the scene transition.

cliff_path - This leads towards the harbour which isn't made yet but it will be an important part of the story later on.
